Good questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a DIY garage?

A shop where you rent the space and the equipment and do the work yourself. At Bridger Bayworks you get a bay with a professional lift, quality tools, and a crew nearby if you need help.

How much does it cost?

Lift and shop time is $40 an hour. Tool access and impact guns are $5 an hour each. Used oil disposal is free and coolant disposal is $5.

Do I need my own tools?

No. Toolbox access is on site for $5 an hour, plus 1/2" and 3/8" impact guns. Bring what you have and use ours for the rest.

What vehicles can I bring?

Cars, trucks, and SUVs. All makes and models are welcome.

Can someone help me if I get stuck?

Yes. Work at your own pace with our knowledgeable crew nearby if you need help.

When are you open?

7 days a week. Hours may vary, so reserve your bay ahead of time.

What do I do with used oil and coolant?

Leave it with us. Used oil disposal is free and coolant disposal is $5.

Is there a rate for regulars?

Yes. If you use the shop more than 15 hours a month, month after month, lift and shop time drops to $35 an hour.

How do I reserve a bay?

Book online, call or text 406-233-6598, or stop by 201 S Weaver St, Building A in Belgrade.

Where is the shop?

201 S Weaver St, Building A, Belgrade, MT 59714. A short drive from Bozeman, Manhattan, and Three Forks.
